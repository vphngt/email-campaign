# **E-Commerce Email Campaign Analytics & Optimization**

**An A/B/C testing and customer segmentation analysis that evaluates the causal revenue impact of targeted email variants.**

---

## Data Source
* **E-Commerce Marketing Dataset**: Analyzed historical customer transaction records.


## Technologies Used
* **Python (Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Scikit-learn)**
* **Jupyter Notebook**


## Results
* **+$25.5K Revenue Lift**: Generated $25.5K in total incremental revenue across marketing treatments over the control.
* **Double the Efficiency**: The top-performing creative boosted click rates from **10.6% to 18.3%** and more than doubled revenue per user (from **$0.65 to $1.42**).
* **Simplified Strategy**: Proved specialized segmentation only offered a negligible **1% gain** over a blanket strategy, leading to a recommendation to send the best-performing email to everyone.
